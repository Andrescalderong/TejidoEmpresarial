including additive manufacturing systems, enterprise sys-

are proposed to be added to the distributed ledger, one or

transaction records, such as via a consensus algorithm.

e.g., through any consensus algorithm, the proposed trans-
action is added to each copy of the distributed ledger across
all the nodes.
