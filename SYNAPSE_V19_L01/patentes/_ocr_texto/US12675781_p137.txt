a neural network.

build a three-dimensional representation of the object in a 30

purpose, multi-purpose, and other classes of robots. Such a
platform that can securely deliver reliable contracted ser-
vices is one key to unlocking the value creation potential of
 25 autonomous robotics. This value proposition may be ampli-

intelligence; when the platform is equipped with intelligence
