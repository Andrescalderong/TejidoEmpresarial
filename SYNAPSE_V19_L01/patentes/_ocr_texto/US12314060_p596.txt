As will be discussed in greater detail below, as discussed
throughout and referring also at least to the example imple-
mentations of FIGS. 173-185, a VCN process, such as VCN
process 10, may receive 18500, by a value chain network
digital twin, information associated with a value chain
network, wherein the information may include a virtual
representation of a plurality of associations between physi-
cal data items of the value chain network, and wherein the
information may be dynamic, real-time and time-phased.
Generally, time-phased may be described as the representa-
tion showing the associations between data items over time
and that the information provided may be used to track the
evolution of the value chain network over time. For
example, simply put, a value chain network digital twin
(which may include at least a portion of VCN process 10)
may be a representation of the various associations between
the data objects of the physical (and often multi-enterprise)
supply chain, as a basis for local and end-to-end aligned
decision making Put another way, value chain network
digital twins may represent a set of value chain network
entities (such as those described throughout). The digital
twins may be configured to simulate properties, states,
operations, behaviors and other aspects of the value chain
network entities. The digital twins may have a visual user
interface, e.g., in the form of 3D models, or may consist of
system specifications or ontologies describing the architec-
ture, including components and their interfaces, of the value
chain network entities. The digital twins may include con-
figuration data or condition data of the value chain network
entities, including data records of the past and current state
ofthe value chain network entities, such as captured through
sensors, through user input, and/or determined by outputs of
AI-based models (e.g., Al-based behavioral models) that
describe the behavior of the value chain network entities.
The digital twins may be updated continuously or periodi-
cally with dynamic, real-time and time-phased data to reflect
the current condition of the value chain network entities,
based on sensor data, test and inspection results, conducted
maintenance, modifications, etc. The digital twins may also
be configured to communicate with a user via multiple
communication channels, such as speech, text, gestures, and
the like. For example, a digital twin may receive queries
from a user about the value chain network entities, generate
responses for the queries, and communicate such responses
to the user. Additionally and/or alternatively, digital twins
