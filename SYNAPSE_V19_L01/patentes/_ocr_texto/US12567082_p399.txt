of special purpose robots, multi-purpose robots, rigid and/or

welding robot, or optionally a multi-purpose robot config-

the like. These roles may not be separately defined in a job

inspection reporting) may lead to such robot role assign-
ment. In embodiments, the fleet configuration system 12020

equipment, and the like. A fleet configuration system may
take into consideration human safety when designating a
human as a team member. As an example, a human team
member may be required to wear a safety face shield when
participating on a team that is performing welding opera-
tions.
