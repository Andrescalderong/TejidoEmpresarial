that the user has quantum access to the training vector data
in a quantum memory. Further, it may be assumed that each
training vector is stored in the quantum memory in terms of
its difference from the class means. These QPCA can then be
applied to provide for dimension reduction using the calcu-
lational benefits of a quantum method.
