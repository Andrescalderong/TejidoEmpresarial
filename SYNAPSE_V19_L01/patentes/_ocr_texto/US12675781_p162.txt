involve entities, what inputs and outputs are exchanged
between entities, and the like), what rules or policies govern
the entities, and the like. The platform 604 may include a set
of entity interaction management systems 1902, which may
comprise one or more artificial intelligence systems (includ-
ing any of the types described throughout this disclosure) for

discovered through entity discovery 1900, including ones
among entities based on how entities have been managed by
human supervisors or by other systems.
