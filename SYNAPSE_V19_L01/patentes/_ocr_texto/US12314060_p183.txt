BACKGROUND
Historically, many of the various categories of goods
purchased and used by household consumers, by businesses
and by other customers were been supplied mainly through
a relatively linear fashion, in which manufacturers and other 20
suppliers of finished goods, components, and other items
handed off items to shipping companies, freight forwarders
and the like, who delivered them to warehouses for tempo-
rary storage, to retailers, where customers purchased them,
or directly to customer locations. Manufacturers and retail- 25
ers undertook various sales and marketing activities to
encourage and meet demand by customers, including
designing products, positioning them on shelves and in
advertising, setting prices, and the like.
