Robot operating unit responsiveness to aggregated
remote-control signals (e.g., instructions or set of instruc-
tions) may be based on a wide range of fleet intelligence
capabilities, knowledge, priorities, goals, and the like. In
general, use of platform-based and/or robot operating unit-

pendent decision-making capabilities for individual robot
