for complex pattern classification of one or more items,
phenomena, modes, states, or the like. In embodiments, a
multilayered feedforward neural network may be trained by
an optimization technique, such as a genetic algorithm, such
as to explore a large and complex space of options to find an
optimum, or near-optimum, global solution. For example,
one or more genetic algorithms may be used to train a
multilayered feedforward neural network to classify com-
plex phenomena, such as to recognize complex operational
modes of industrial machines, such as modes involving
complex interactions among machines (including interfer-
ence effects, resonance effects, and the like), modes involv-
ing non-linear phenomena, such as impacts ofvariable speed
shafts,which may make analysis of vibration and other

many others, such as in refrigeration systems, refining
systems, reactor systems, catalytic systems, and others.
