then automatically trains and add new hidden units one by
one, creating a multi-layer structure. Once a new hidden unit
has been added to the network, its input-side weights may be
frozen. This unit then becomes a permanent feature-detector
in the network, available for producing outputs or for 65
creating other, more complex feature detectors. The cascade-
