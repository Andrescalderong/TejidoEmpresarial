In embodiments, the PMCP system enables communicat-
ing vectorized information together with model parameters
that allow predictive models on a receiving end to predict
probabilities of future values. The vectorized information
may be transmitted and processed to determine a number of 1o i
probability-based states. For example, motion vectors and
model parameters for predicting future locations based on
motion vectors may be transmitted using PMCP, and a
receiving location may use the motion vectors as inputs to a
parameterized predictive model (e.g., a model that deter- 15
mines future locations of an item using the model param-
eters), which may generate probabilities that an item asso-
ciated with a motion vector is in different locations. As
another example, the PMCP system may support commu-
parameters that allow current and/or future sensor readings
to be predicted. Applied in an environment with large
numbers of sensors with different accuracies and reliabili-
ties, the probabilistic vector-based mechanism of the PMCP
system allows large numbers, if not all, data streams to be 25
used to produce refined models representing the current
 state, past states and likely future states of value chain items
(e.g, goods, services, and/or the like). Approximation meth-
ods may include importance sampling, and the resulting
predictive model may be a particle filter, condensation 30
algorithm, Monte Carlo localization, or other suitable mod-
els.

In embodiments, security systems may generate and
transmit vectors showing changes in state, as unusual events
tend to cause one or more vectors to show unusual patterns.
In a security setting, detecting multiple simultaneous 50
unusual vectors may trigger escalation and a response by, for

cation security concern is around the protection of stored
data, and in a vector-based system data may not need to be 55
stored (or may be stored on fewer devices), so the risk of
data loss is removed or reduced.

the parameterized models to generate data for further use by
the receiving device.
