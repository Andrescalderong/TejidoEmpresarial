In some implementations, VCN process 10 may include
the ability to synchronize planning and predictive sourcing,
which may be two components of supply chain management
costs. For instance, VCN process 1o's synchronized plan-
ning may be generally described as the process of aligning
some or all of the activities and functions within the supply
(or demand) chain to work together towards a common goal. 30
This may include, e.g., demand planning/sensing for highly
accurate forecasts, production planning, inventory manage-
ment, and logistics planning By VCN process 10 coordinat-
ing these activities, companies may ensure that they are
efficiently using their resources to meet customer demand
while minimizing costs. As an example, as will be discussed
below, if a company knows that demand for a particular
product is going to increase, they can use VCN process 10's
synchronized planning to ensure that they have enough
inventory on hand to meet that demand, while also ensuring 40
that production and logistics are prepared to handle the
increased volume. VCN process 10's predictive sourcing
may be generally described as the use of data analytics and
predictive modeling to forecast future demand and identify
potential supply chain disruptions. By VCN process 10
analyzing historical data and current trends, companies can
use VCN process 10 to identify potential risks and take
proactive measures to mitigate them. For example, if a
company knows (e.g., via VCN process 10) that a particular
supplier has a history of delivery delays, they can use VCN 50
process 10's predictive sourcing to identify the likelihood of
future delays and take steps to ensure that they have alter-
native suppliers lined up or extra inventory on hand to avoid
any disruption in the supply chain.
