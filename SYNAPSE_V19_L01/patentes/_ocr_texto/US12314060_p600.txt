data-as-a-service may generally refer to the practice of
providing access to data (such as value chain network digital
twin data) as a service, which may be integrated into
different stages of the value chain. Value chain network
digital twins may consist of a series of interconnected
activities that create value for customers, and these activities
may include everything from raw material sourcing and
production to marketing and distribution. By providing
access to Al-based data as a service, businesses may enhance
improving efficiency, reducing costs, and ultimately deliv-
ering better products or services to customers.

can predict demand for a particular product. This data may
be integrated into the planning stage of the digital twin value 25

anticipate customer demand and adjust production accord-
ingly. Similarly, the digital twin value chain data that ana-

better understand customer preferences and tailor marketing
campaigns to specific customer segments. Overall, data-as-
a-service may help businesses in value chain network digital
twin to stay competitive by leveraging the power of Al-
based data (e.g., digital twin value chain Al-based training
