process only shows shipping entities that are involved in the
process. In this example, the digital twin visualization
module 13322 may retrieve a digital twin of the process, as

13322 may then render each of the digital twins (e.g-, the

may perform the process on the rendered digital twins. It is
noted that, as a process may be performed over a period of

sequential frames that demonstrate the process. In this
scenario, the movements of the shipping entities implicated

iors defined in the respective digital twins of the machines
and/or devices.
