[1408]   In embodiments, cascade correlation may be used
as an architecture and supervised learning algorithm, supple-
menting adjustment of the weights in a network of fixed
topology. Cascade-correlation may begin with a minimal
network, then automatically trains and adds new hidden
units one by one, creating a multi-layer structure. Once anew
hidden unit has been added to the network, its input-side
weights may be frozen. This unit then becomes a permanent
feature-detector in the network, available for producing
outputs or for creating other, more complex feature detec-
tors. The cascade-correlation architecture may learn quickly,
determine its own size and topology, and retain the struc-
tures it has built even if the training set changes and requires
no back-propagation.
