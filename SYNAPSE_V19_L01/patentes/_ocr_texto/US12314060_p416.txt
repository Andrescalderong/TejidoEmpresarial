12306D. The workflow definitions 12306D define at least

project and/or job, including any loops, iterations, triggering

tion system 12018 may determine the workflows 12306D
based on the task definitions 12304D that comprise a job
and/or project. The job configuration system 12018 may
leverage libraries of preconfigured workflows to complete
certain jobs. Additionally, or alternatively, the job configu-

intelligence layer 12004 to obtain an initial workflow defi-
