In embodiments, the additive manufacturing unit 10102
may involve the use of an inkjet type printhead to deliver a
liquid or colloidal binder material to layers of a powdered
buildmaterial.The printing techniquemay involve applying
a layer of a powdered build material to a surface, such as
using a roller. After the build material is applied to the
surface, the printhead delivers the liquid binder to predeter-
mined areas of the layer of material. The binder infiltrates
the material and reacts with the powder, causing the layer to
solidify in the printed areas by, for example, activating an
adhesive in the powder. After the first cross-sectional portion
is formed, the steps are repeated, and successive cross-
sectional portions are fabricated until the final product is
formed.
