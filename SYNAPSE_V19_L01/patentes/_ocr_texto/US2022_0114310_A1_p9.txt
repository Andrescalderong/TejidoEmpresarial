able storage media. It is further noted that, now and/or in the
near future: (i) external device(s) 214 may be able to supply,

external to subsystem 102 may be able to provide memory

210: (i) store data in a manner that is less transient than a
signal in transit; and (i) store data on a tangible medium
(such as magnetic or optical domains). In this embodiment,
memory 208 is volatile storage, while persistent storage 210
provides nonvolatile storage. The media used by persistent
storage 210 may also be removable. For example, a remov-
able hard drive may be used for persistent storage 210. Other
examples include optical and magnetic disks, thumb drives,
and smart cards that are inserted into a drive for transfer onto
another computer-readable storage medium that is also part
of persistent storage 210.
