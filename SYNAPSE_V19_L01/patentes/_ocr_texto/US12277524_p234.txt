variables for which may be altered or varied to produce

twin simulation system 8116 may return the simulation
results to the COO digital twin 8306, which in turn outputs

ing to different operational parameter configurations. In
embodiments, an executive agent trained by the user may
select the parameter sets based on the various outcomes.
