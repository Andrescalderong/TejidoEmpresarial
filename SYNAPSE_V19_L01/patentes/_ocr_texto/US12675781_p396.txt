and a set of acceptable outcomes to drive optimization
algorithms that manage thermal flows in a multi-purpose
robot 12100. This could be used to actively manage thermal
conditions or optimize heat transfer to maintain acceptable
operating conditions. In embodiments, the thermal manage-
ment service 12161 may help reclaim waste heat energy. For
example, waste heat could be moved to actively cool hotter
components, used with emerging nanoscale or other ther-
moelectric devices, etc. In embodiments, thermal manage-
ment service 12161 may leverage robot sensor data, task
