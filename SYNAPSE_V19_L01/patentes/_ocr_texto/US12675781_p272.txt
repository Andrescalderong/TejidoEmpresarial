In embodiments, a CHRO digital twin 8316 may depict a
data item with an icon indicating whether the data item is at
a normal state, a suboptimal state, a critical state, or an alarm
state. In embodiments, the icons may be different colors, 35 t
fonts, symbols, codes or the like. For example, a CHRO
digital twin 8316 may depict high performer turnover rate
data with an orange icon indicating that the high performer
turnover rate is at a critical level. Continuing the example,
an HR executive may be enabled to escalate the high 40
performer turnover rate data to another executive, such as
the CEO, via the CHRO digital twin 8316. In embodiments,
a CHRO digital twin 8316 may automatically highlight data
items that are at suboptimal, critical, or alarm state.
