In embodiments, the modeling circuit 9424 may generate
a model based on the prioritized governance requirements as
determined by the prioritization circuit 9422. For example,
if the highest priority governance requirement is a set of
safety requirements, then the generated model may initially
check for safety violations or apply other safety governance
requirements. Then, if the second highest priority gover-
nance requirement is a set of regulatory governance require-
ments, the model may, after enforcing safety governance,
enforce regulatory governance. In some cases (e.g., due to
conflicts), the model may omit certain governance require-
ments from the model (e.g-, a quality requirement that
conflicts with a safety requirement). In this way, the mod-
eling circuit 9424 may generate a model specifying a flow
for enforcing governance on a data set. The modeling circuit
9424 may cause the generated model to reference various
digital twins from digital twin library 9454 that specify
information about one or more environments, networks,
systems, or the like, to retrieve various data that may be
necessary for checking and enforcement.
