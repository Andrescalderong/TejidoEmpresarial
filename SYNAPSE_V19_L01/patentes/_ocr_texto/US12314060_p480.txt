In embodiments, the properties of a digital twin may be
adjusted. For example, the temperature of a digital twin, the 40
humidity of a digital twin, the shape of a digital twin, the
material of a digital twin, the dimensions of a digital twin,
or any other suitable parameters may be adjusted. As the
properties of the digital twin are adjusted, other properties
may be affected as well. For example, if the temperature 45
within a smart container is increased, the pressure within the
smart container may increase as well, such as a pressure of
a gas in accordance with the ideal gas law. In another
example, if a digital twin of a subzero shipping environment
is increased to above freezing temperatures, the properties of 50
an embedded twin of water in a solid state (i.e., ice) may
change into a liquid state over time.
