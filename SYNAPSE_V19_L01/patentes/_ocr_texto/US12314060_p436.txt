In embodiments, the MPS 12158 may utilize a 3D path
planning algorithm for determining the optimal path. For
example, sampling-based algorithms may determine fea-
sible paths for the robot's motion using information from a
graph that consists of randomly sampled nodes and con-
nected edges in the given configuration space. Such ran-
domized approaches have a strong advantage in terms of
quickly providing solutions to complex problems, such as in
a high-dimensional configuration space. Examples of 3D
path planning algorithms that may be used by the MPS
system include visibility graph, random-exploring algo-
rithms such as rapidly exploring random tree, Probabilistic
Road Map, optimal search algorithms (such as Dijkstra's
algorithm, A* algorithm) and bioinspired planning algo-
rithms.
