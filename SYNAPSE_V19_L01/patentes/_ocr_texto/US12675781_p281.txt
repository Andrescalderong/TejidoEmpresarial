In embodiments, methods and systems described herein
that involve an expert system or self-organization capability
may use a genetic scale recurrent neural network. In such
embodiments, an RNN (often a LSTM) may be used where
a series may be decomposed into a number of scales where
every scale informs the primary length between two con-
secutive points. A first order scale consists of a normal RNN,
a second order consists of all points separated by two indices
and so on. The Nth order RNN connects the first and last
node. The outputs from all the various scales may be treated
as a committee of members, and the associated scores may
be used genetically for the next iteration.
