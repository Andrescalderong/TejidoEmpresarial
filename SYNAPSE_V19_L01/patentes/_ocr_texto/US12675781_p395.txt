against intrusion, dust, accidental contact, and water. An IP

electric equipment and cabinets against solid foreign mat-
ters, which is represented by seven ratings from “"o", which
means no protection against dust entry, to "6", which means
no dust entry inside. The second sign represents a protection
rating against water entry, which is represented by nine 20
ratings from “o", which means no protection against water
entry, to "8", which means the optimum resistance. When no
rating is determined, "X" is denoted.
